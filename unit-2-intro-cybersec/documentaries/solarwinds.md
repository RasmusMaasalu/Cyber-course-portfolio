**Q1 The incident in your own words**
What happened in the SolarWinds incident? Describe it in your own words.
The Solarwinds hack was a state sponsored supply chain attack against the private software company SolarWinds.

**Q2 Who was affected, and how?**
Who were the victims? What was the impact.
The primary target was SolarWinds and it was leveraged to attack other corporations and government organizations which relied on SolarWinds services making the attack far larger than just one company.

**Q3 The CIA principle**
Which leg of the CIA triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability) was most directly attacked in this incident?
Confidentiality and Integrity were the main pillars of this attack. 
 
**Q4 The attack technique - what made this one different**
SolarWinds is often called a "supply chain attack." In your own words, what is a supply chain attack?
A supply attack is when a malicious attacker targets a service provider by hijacking said services with malicious programs which then becomes a risk to any user of that service.

**Q5 How was it discovered**
How did the world find out about the attack?
A cybersecurity services company called FireEye detected an intrusion in its systems and informed SolarWinds of a potential attack.

**Q6 What could have helped**
Name one defence or practice that might have prevented or limited the damage.
A supply chain attack is very difficult to defend against and the best practice is probably making a far stricter integrity check of new software releases and perhaps even looking for discrepencies in release schedules.
